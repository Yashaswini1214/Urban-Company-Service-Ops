# Dashboard Story

## City Ops Lead

**Headline:** Bengaluru has the highest SLA breach rate at 15.9%, making it the clearest city-level SLA priority.

**Evidence:** Bengaluru records 17 SLA breaches across 107 bookings, equal to a 15.9% breach rate. Hyderabad is next at 14.3% (14 breaches across 98 bookings), while Delhi NCR is lowest at 7.2% (6 breaches across 83 bookings).

**Implication:** City Ops should look into the Bengaluru operating pattern first, using Delhi NCR as a lower-breach reference point and checking where missed-SLA events are concentrated before setting corrective actions.

## Category Lead

**Headline:** Deep Home Cleaning is the strongest category, while Salon for Women is comparatively under-booked by volume.

**Evidence:** Deep Home Cleaning generates ₹5,08,964 from 176 bookings, the highest revenue and booking volume of the six active categories. Salon for Women has only 65 bookings, the lowest booking volume, while still generating ₹1,51,689 in revenue.

**Implication:** The Category Lead should protect Deep Home Cleaning's scale while looking into whether Salon for Women has capacity, demand, partner-availability, or conversion constraints that explain its lower booking volume.
